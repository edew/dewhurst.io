This is the recipe that I use for broccoli muffins.

  • 150g self-raising flour
  • 150ml milk
  • 130g broccoli
  • 70g grated cheese
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 0.5 tsp paprika

Steam the broccoli until cooked and chop into small pieces.

Combine all ingradients in a bowl and mix.

Divide evenly between paper baking cups.

Sprinkle a small amount of cheese on top of each muffin.

Bake for 15 to 20 minutes at 180 °C.
